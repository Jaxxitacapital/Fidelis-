A real letter

Dear Fidelis,

I still think about February 4, 2023, because that day did not announce itself loudly. It was just a school day, a hello, a small spark, and then suddenly you became someone my heart remembered.

You were my bestie before you were my girl, and that matters to me. It means our love was not built from pressure or pretending. It grew from laughter, trust, History class, tiny conversations, and the calm feeling of knowing I had found someone true.

Your name means faithful, loyal, and sincere, but you make those words feel alive. You are the smile that softens my day, the memory I replay when I need peace, and the person I keep choosing with patience, honesty, and love.

If this site is a little dramatic, it is because ordinary words did not feel enough. I wanted you to have a whole place that says: you are treasured, you are beautiful, and our story is still my favourite one.

Forever yours,
Eustace ❤️
